CHECKING WHAT THE DOCUMENT ACTUALLY SHOWS

More label matches can mean more unsupported guesses.

We separately evaluated 70 real images labelled by the assistant before consulting Jev predictions or store metadata. A country was observable in 62; eight were indeterminate crops or documents without sufficient location clues. These are assistant judgments, not human ground truth. Prompts were unchanged.

Measurement Original prompt Focused prompt

Image reviews assess the complete OCR-to-country pipeline. They do not isolate OCR errors from Jev errors. Two oversized images failed extraction and remain in the 70-case denominator. Confidence is not proof of visible country evidence.
